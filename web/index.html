Getting started

Open your calculator’s complete ROM file. Its model and settings are detected automatically.

When “Memory Clear” appears, press the top-right key (OK).

Click the calculator keys, or use your keyboard’s numbers, operators and Enter.

Switching away pauses the calculator. Save session exports your current calculator state without its ROM. After reopening the same ROM, Load session restores your work without repeating startup. Changes are not saved automatically.
